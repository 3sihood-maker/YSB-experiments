Paul Challenge Idea

This is not a created prototype, but an idea we could pursue. It is a way to connect a young athlete’s training schedule (real, existing) with an online challenge to be active over a longer period (maybe a month). This way the users can set a training schedule, read about Paul’s missionary journeys, interact with the Bible and track their activity over the period. The goal is to receive a Medal that goes into their “Achievements” listing.
