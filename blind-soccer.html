Blind Soccer

the way Baba plays it

The ball has bells inside it. Nobody on the pitch can see it — they find it by listening.

  • Run — drag on the left, or the arrow keys.
  • Listen — the bells sound from where the ball is.
  • The guide taps wood behind the goal: that is the way to shoot.
  • Kick — hold to build power, let go to strike.

The defender calls “voy!” as he comes in — the real rule, so nobody collides.
