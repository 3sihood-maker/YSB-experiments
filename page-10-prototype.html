Does the New Testament say anything about …

Actually, it does! Many of the writers mention running, wrestling, and training as ways to understand how we grow in faith.

One writer, the apostle Paul, often used sports examples to explain what it means to follow Jesus. He wrote to believers about running races, training with discipline, and keeping our eyes on the goal. As you read, look for highlighted segments that might help you think in new ways about your life both on and off the field.

Sports can show us who we really are—the good, the bad, and the messy. God uses all of that to grow us. And sports remind us that following Jesus takes commitment, focus, and a heart that doesn’t give up.
