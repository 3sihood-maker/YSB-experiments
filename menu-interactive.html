Interactive
Prototypes

These experiments are ones that seek to use interactivity as a tool to engage with the Bible. Also using sport and sport metaphors as ways to shape the content.

Start with these three

Word Ball

Using a Soccer Ball and Volleyball to display words with verses underneath. Could be used for any interaction, question or link to a Bible Passage. Could also use any sport ball.

 11

Bible Sizes

Exploring the idea of reading the Bible in a sports field. It gives relative sizes of some Bible buildings, distances and heights, compared to sport fields and playing areas. It takes into account the Hebrew and Greek differences, though needs to be fully checked for accuracy.

 12

Run the Race

Playing with sports imagery on an animated page. Lots of potential for ideas using this tech.

Build the Ultimate Teammate

A prototype with placeholder text to build up concepts we want to link with Bible passages but done as a team.

 14 Example 15

RONDO

The goal was to encourage group reflection throughout the week rather than just at church or bible study. It encourages you to share a reflection, giving a variety of options, and then encourages you to pass it on.

 15 Example 16

Jesus’s Classroom

This meshes teachable moments and experiential learning with the Bible to engage youth. It is an interactive way to learn to reflect on lessons of life and apply to Scripture. The app asks questions to keep digging deeper.

Wonder Trail

A knowledge graph prototype allows for digging deeply into a passage. The layout is not good yet, just a test of what’s possible.
