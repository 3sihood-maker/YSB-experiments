Snowboarding

Q:What has snowboarding taught you about life?

S:Snowboarding has taught me to persevere even though it can be challenging sometimes. That goes for snowboarding as well as for my life in general. Once I was going down an icy slope when a skier clipped me and his ski dug into my ankle. I got badly hurt. I persevered through the day but only with the help of God quieting my emotions and allowing me to be calm.

Q:You have a goal to avoid comparison with others. Why?

S:I used to compare myself with others because I was smaller than others and overweight, but then I went to a Christian camp and realized that it doesn’t matter what other people think but to play to the best of the ability that God has given me. I’m learning that it is easy to compare myself to others in other areas of my life and sport is teaching me this is not wise.

Q:Tell us about a Bible verse that has helped you?

S:Matthew 22:37–39: “Jesus answered: ‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind.’ This is the first and most important commandment. The second most important commandment is like this one. And it is, ‘Love others as much as you love yourself.’ ” These verses stood out to me because sport is a practical challenge to do these things.

Q:How does knowing Jesus help you on the slopes?

S:Knowing that I’m not doing it for people—but instead for God—who is right there with me in the snow.

Think about this:

One of the things Jesus told his followers was, “I will be with you always, even until the end of the world.” (Matthew 28:20b)

What does that mean for you?
