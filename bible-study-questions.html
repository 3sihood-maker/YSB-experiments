Simple Bible Study
Discussion Questions

Four moves for reading any passage — on your own, with a mate, or with a group. Same shape every time, so the questions get out of the way and the passage does the work.

01 Warm Up

  • Pray to God to ask for help reading his word.
  • Ask What do I remember from the last passage? What did I learn?
  • Read the passage.
  • Ask What do I notice? What surprises or confuses me?

02 Training Drills

  • What does the passage say about God? About Jesus? About people?
  • Is there anything that’s hard to hear? Why?
  • Where do I see this in me? Where do I see it in the world?
  • What does this mean for me?

03 Refining My Technique

  • What have I learnt?
  • How am I going to live this out — on and off the field?

04 Cool Down

  • Pray to God about it. You might want to:
  • Thank him for something you read.
  • Say sorry for anything the passage has reminded you of.
  • Ask for his help living his way.
On the cool-down prayer: other ways to remember it are TSP — Thanks, Sorry, Please — or ACTS — Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication. ACTS is more traditional, but the language may be a stretch for younger readers.
