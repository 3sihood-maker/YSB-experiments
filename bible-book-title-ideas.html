Bible Book Title Ideas

Two title treatments for books of the Bible, built out of torn paper rather than set in a typeface. The idea is that the name of the book is not printed on the page — it is cut through it, so the layers underneath do the telling.

Tap either artboard to see it full size.

Why torn and not set

A typeface gives every book of the Bible the same voice. Torn paper does not — the material under the letters changes what the name feels like before anyone has read a word of it. Exodus gets desert and water; Joshua gets a pitch.

Where this would go

Book openers in a study series, chapter cards in a video, or the cover of a season. The artboards are square-ish and full bleed, so they crop to a wide banner or a phone screen without losing the word.

What is not decided yet

Two books does not make a system. The open questions are whether every book gets figures (Joshua has them, Exodus does not), whether the palette is per-book or per-testament, and what a book with no obvious landscape — a letter, say — would be torn out of.
