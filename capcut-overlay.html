CapCut Overlay

A test of putting a digital layer over a real printed page. The camera is handheld on an open Bible at Mark 1, and a highlight sits on the opening verse — staying with the words as the page and the shot move.

14 seconds · sound on · portrait
↓ Download the MP4

What it shows

The highlighted phrase is “the Son of God, as is written in Isaiah the prophet” — Mark 1:1–2. The overlay is tracked to the text rather than pinned to the frame, so it holds its place on the printed line while the book is being held.

That is the thing worth testing: whether a physical Bible and a phone screen can be the same surface, so a printed page can carry the kind of marking, colour and motion a screen does.

Made in

CapCut, on a phone. The last couple of seconds are the app’s own outro, left in as it came.
