Where it happened

Bible Story Location Finder

Type a passage or a name. If the place is well established, it opens on Google Maps. If it isn't, this says so rather than guessing.

    How to read the ratings

    • Established. The site is identified and not seriously disputed. The pin is where the story happened, as far as anyone can tell.
    • Debated. There is a leading candidate — usually a long tradition, sometimes hard evidence — but it is contested. The pin shows it, and the note says what the argument is.
    • We are not sure. No accepted location. No pin. Where sites have been proposed, they are listed as proposals and labelled as such.

    About

    Every location is stored inside this page. There is no server, no account, no API key and nothing to renew — it will work in ten years exactly as it works today, and it works offline apart from Google Maps itself, which needs a connection to draw the map.

    Coordinates were checked against published gazetteers and site reports, including OpenBible.info's Bible geocoding data, the Pleiades gazetteer and excavation reports. Where sources disagreed, the entry was downgraded rather than tidied up.

    On your phone: open this page in Safari or Chrome, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. It behaves like an app after that.
